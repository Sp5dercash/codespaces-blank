The game im making is based off of a game called return man, which is you trying to get to the other
endzone without trying to get tackled. The player will have to try and dodge the CPU unless the game
will end.what makes it challenging each time you complete a level there will be more defenders.
each time and will be a different weather which will change the field. Like snow,mud puddles
or even holes in the ground.


There will be a select level screen which makes you choose a level, you can only do level one until
you complete it.


My game objects will be The Return Man (Player),The Football,The Yellow Target Circle,Defenders / Tacklers,
Teamates (Blockers),Lightning Bolts,The Touchdown Zone (End Zone),Field Lines & Boundary Markers.


The controll schemes will be the four arrows or you can use W,A,S,D. Those buttons will have you move
The returner.


The enemy will be the defenders which are trying to physically stop you from scoring, Some obstacle
objects will be the snow which will have you sliding a little, and also some lightning bolts that
will give you a timed speed boost.


When you cross the endzone you win and when you get tackled by a defender the game will end.
