My game will be about football, you will have to try and return the kickoff from your endzone to the other.
Once you get the ball you will have 6 blockers out in front and there will be 7 defenders trying to prevent
you from scoring. Once you score you will get to move on to the next level which gets harder each time.


The controls will be the up, down, right, and left arrows to move your character. The space bar will be used
to do some type of skill move like (hurdle, juke, or spin).





The game primary meschanics are, Ai blocking and Defender logic, Core Player Mechanics, and Level Progression & Risk-Reward

My game primary goals is to catch a kicked football and run it past a designated target yard line—or 
all the way into the end zone for a touchdown—without getting tackled or stepping out of bounds.


The obstacles in my game is active defenders trying to tackle you and environmental hazards that change how the field plays.
Also trying not to run out od bounds is an obstacles

The Aesthetic of my game is a bold, punchy, retro-arcade
